Book Korean restaurants without a Korean number: CatchTable Global and Naver (2026)

English日本語繁體中文

Popular Korean restaurants run bookings and waitlists through apps that used to need a Korean number. Two routes now work for visitors.

Checked 17 Nov 2026 · What changed

Check your own setup

With a home number or data-only eSIM

Works

CatchTable Global and Naver passport verification both work without a Korean number.

  1. CatchTable Global: sign up by email, pay deposits with a foreign card.
  2. Naver Map bookings: verify with your passport (available since 4 Jun 2026).

If that fails: Not every restaurant is on these apps. Ask staff to add you to the waitlist in person.

With a Korean prepaid 010 number

Works

CatchTable Global and Naver passport verification both work without a Korean number.

  2. Naver Map bookings: verify with your passport (available since 4 Jun 2026).

With a Korean number in your name (ARC)

Works

All booking apps work, including the local CatchTable app and Naver.

  1. Naver Map or CatchTable with your verified number.

If that fails: CatchTable Global if the local app gives you trouble.
