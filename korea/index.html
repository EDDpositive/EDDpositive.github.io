    Your first 24 hours in Korea, in order

    The trip goes wrong in the first few hours: at the airport, in the taxi, at check-in. Do these in order.

    1. Before you flySubmit the e-Arrival Card online in the 3 days before you land (e-arrivalcard.go.kr). Check whether your nationality needs a K-ETA (k-eta.go.kr).
    2. Before you flyInstall the apps on your checklist and set them up on home Wi-Fi.
    3. On landingSwitch on your eSIM data before you leave the plane, so maps and messages work at immigration.
    4. At the airportBuy a T-money card at an airport convenience store and top it up with cash. On iPhone, Mobile T-money is an option.
    5. Into the cityTake the airport railroad (AREX) or an airport bus, or call a taxi with k.ride or Uber.
    6. At your stayShow your host's address card to the driver. If your host sent you a link, it's at the top of this page.
    7. First eveningVerify Naver with your passport and set up CatchTable Global, so dinner bookings work.
