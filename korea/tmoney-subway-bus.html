T-money with a foreign card: paying for the subway and bus in Korea (2026)

English日本語繁體中文

Korean subways and buses run on transit cards. Topping one up with a foreign card is now partly possible on iPhone. Cash always works.

Checked 17 Nov 2026 · What changed

Check your own setup

With a home number or data-only eSIM · iPhone

Works with a workaround

On iPhone, Mobile T-money can now top up with some foreign cards. Not every card brand is supported.

  1. Install Mobile T-money and add a transit card.
  2. Try topping up with your card. If your card brand isn't accepted, use the backup below.

If that fails: Buy a physical T-money card at a convenience store and top it up with cash at the counter or station machines.

With a home number or data-only eSIM · Android

Works with a workaround

Foreign-card top-up in the Android T-money app was not available at our last check.

  1. Buy a physical T-money card at a convenience store.
  2. Top up with cash at the counter or at station machines.

If that fails: T-money says a separate app, Mobile Korea Tour Card, supports foreign cards.

With a Korean number in your name (ARC)

Works

With a Korean card, mobile or physical T-money works normally.

  1. Add T-money in your wallet app or use a transit-enabled Korean card.

If that fails: Physical T-money with cash always works.
